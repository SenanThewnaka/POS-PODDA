1. Description of Goods & Services

POS Podda provides a cloud-based Point of Sale (POS) and Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) Software-as-a-Service (SaaS). We offer digital subscriptions (Plus and Pro tiers) that grant access to our platform for managing inventory, tracking sales, printing receipts, and managing customer credit.

Note on Electrical Goods: We do not sell hardware or electrical goods. Users must source their own compatible mobile devices and thermal printers.

2. Delivery & Shipping Policy

Since POS Podda is a digital Software-as-a-Service product, no physical shipping is involved. Upon successful payment authorization, the subscription is activated instantly, and the user's account is upgraded immediately over the internet. There are no shipping delays, import/export restrictions, or customs duties applicable to our digital service.

3. Return, Refund & Cancellation Policy

Refunds: All subscription payments are final. Due to the digital nature of the service, we operate on a "No Refund" policy for partial months or unused time. We encourage users to test the platform extensively on the Free/Trial tier before upgrading.

Cancellations: You can cancel your recurring subscription at any time directly through the "Settings" menu inside the POS Podda application. Cancellation stops future charges, but your premium access will remain active until the end of your current paid billing cycle.

4. Data Privacy Policy

What we collect: We collect shop owner details (name, email) and transaction data generated within the POS Podda app (sales, inventory logs).

Why we collect it: This data is strictly used to provide the POS Podda service, generate your financial reports, and maintain your account security.

Third Parties: Your payment details are processed securely by Payments.lk and are never stored on our servers. We do not sell or share your business data with unauthorized third parties.

Restricting Use: Users have the right to request full account deletion via the application settings or by contacting customer support.

5. Transaction Security & Currency

Security: All ordering and payment processes are secured via Payments.lk using 128-bit Secure Socket Layer (SSL) encryption. This ensures your credit card data is encrypted during transmission, protecting you from unauthorized access.

Currency: All transactions are processed in LKR (Sri Lankan Rupee).
